Our core service

Snacks and cold drinks, done properly

Snack and drink vending is what we do. Not a sideline to a coffee business — the main thing, and the thing we've built our stocking and service routine around.

The machine goes in free for qualifying sites, we fill it with a range chosen for your type of site, and then we adjust it. Lines that sell get more facings. Lines that sit there get replaced. Within a month or two the machine looks quite different from the day it arrived, because it's been shaped by your people.

  • Recognised brands, not unknown discount lines
  • Chilled drinks held at proper temperature year round
  • Stock rotated on date, every visit

What goes in

A range built around your site

A warehouse on early shifts and a gym reception want very different things. We start from the category, then refine from the data.

Crisps & savoury

The reliable backbone of any machine — standard and grab bags, plus nuts and savoury snacks for sites that want them.

Chocolate & confectionery

The names everyone recognises, in singles and duos. Consistently the highest-turnover shelf on most sites.

Cold drinks

Chilled bottles and cans — colas, water, juice and sugar-free options, all held at drinking temperature.

Energy drinks

The single strongest seller on depots, estates and night-shift sites. Full sugar and sugar-free, in cans and bottles.

Cereal & protein bars

For gyms, clinics and sites that want a lighter option on the shelf alongside the chocolate.

Chilled food & meal deals

On larger sites, a chilled unit can carry sandwiches and light meals. Available where the volume supports it.

Keeping it full

Restocking planned around real demand

An empty machine is worse than no machine — people try it twice, find nothing, and stop looking. So we don't restock on a lazy fixed rota.

We track what each machine sells and when, then schedule visits to land before the popular rows empty. Busy sites are typically serviced weekly or twice weekly; quieter ones less often. Either way, the schedule follows the sales.

  • Sales monitored per machine, not per region
  • Peak periods anticipated — shift changes, breaks, summer
  • Date rotation and machine cleaning on every visit
  • Slow lines swapped out rather than left to sit

Faults dealt with by us

Refunds without the argument
