Kent coverage

Built for how Kent actually works

Kent is a logistics county. The Dartford crossing corridor, the Medway industrial estates, the distribution parks along the M20 and M2, the trade counters and vehicle depots that keep the freight moving — these are sites full of people working long, awkward hours a long way from anywhere selling a sandwich.

That's ideal vending territory, and it's badly served. A lot of Kent sites are either on a national contract nobody can get hold of, or they've given up on vending entirely after a bad experience.

  • Coverage across north, west and mid Kent, plus Medway
  • Free machine, install and servicing for qualifying sites
  • Stock built around shift patterns, not office hours
  • Local operator — we're the ones who turn up

Kent FAQs

Questions from Kent sites

Are you actually local, or London-based with a Kent page?

Fair question, and one worth asking any operator. We run a single connected route across London and Kent, and Kent sites are serviced on that route — not treated as an occasional long-distance trip.

Our site is gated with security sign-in. Is that a problem?

No — it's normal on Kent logistics sites. We just need a workable arrangement for restock access. We'll agree it before install rather than discover it afterwards.

We run 24-hour shifts. Can the machine be available all night?

Yes, and those are often the best-performing sites. Placed in a secure staff area, a machine can run round the clock — night shifts frequently outsell the day.

Do you cover east Kent — Canterbury, Dover, Thanet?

Our core route currently runs to Ashford and the Medway towns. For sites further east, get in touch — if the site is strong enough we'll look at extending the route.

Is the machine free in Kent too?

Yes — identical terms to London. Free machine, free install, free servicing for qualifying sites. See free vending machines for the detail.
