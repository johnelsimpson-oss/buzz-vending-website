Questions

The things sites ask us first

Is the machine really free?

Yes. For qualifying sites there's no purchase cost, no rental and no service charge. We fund, own and run the machine. All you provide is the floor space and a standard 13A socket.

How many people do we need on site?

As a rule of thumb, around 15 or more people on site regularly. But it depends more on how long they're there and what else is nearby — a 20-person depot with no shop for a mile beats a 60-person office above a Tesco. Ask us and we'll assess it properly.

Who pays for the electricity?

The site does — it's a standard socket and a modern machine is not a heavy draw. Everything else is ours.

How often do you restock?

It's set by what the machine actually sells. Busy sites are typically serviced weekly or twice weekly; quieter ones less often. We monitor the sales rather than guessing, so restocks are planned before rows run empty.

Can we choose what goes in it?

Absolutely. We'll start with a range built around your type of site, then adjust it from real sales data. If your team wants more healthy lines, fewer fizzy drinks, or a particular brand adding, just tell us.

What if it breaks or gets jammed?

You message us and we deal with it. Repairs, parts, refunds for failed vends and machine insurance are all our responsibility.

Are we locked into a long contract?

No lock-in traps. We put the arrangement in writing so both sides know where they stand, with a straightforward notice period. If it isn't working for you, we take the machine away.

Find out if your site qualifies

Tell us where you are and roughly how many people are on site. We'll come back with a straight yes or no — and if it's a yes, we can usually install within a couple of weeks.
