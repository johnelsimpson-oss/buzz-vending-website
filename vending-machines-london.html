London FAQs

Questions from London sites

Do you charge extra for central London?

No. Our free-loan terms are the same across the whole coverage area. Access and delivery windows matter more than postcode — we'll cover those at the site visit.

Can you deliver into a congestion or ULEZ zone?

Yes. Those costs are ours, not yours, and they're already factored into how we run the route.

Our building has restricted delivery hours. Is that a problem?

Very common in London and usually fine. Tell us the permitted windows and goods-lift arrangements and we'll build the service visits around them.

We're a multi-tenant building — who needs to agree?

Normally the managing agent or building facilities team, since the machine usually goes in a shared area. If you can point us to the right contact, we'll handle the conversation.

How quickly can you install?

Typically within a couple of weeks of the site visit, subject to the machine type and your building's access arrangements.
